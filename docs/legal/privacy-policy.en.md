## 2. Information we collect

The App does not use advertising identifiers and does not track you across apps or websites.

### 2.1 Account information

Collected only if you create an account. You can browse tourist spots, the map, evacuation shelters and etiquette guidance without an account.

- Email address
- Password (we never hold it in plain text; it is stored as a hash by our authentication provider)
- Display name
- Profile icon image
- If you sign in with Google, the email address and basic profile information Google provides

### 2.2 Content you submit

- Review text, rating and submission time for tourist spots

Reviews are shown publicly together with your display name.

### 2.3 Location data

- Your device's current location, only if you grant the operating system's location permission

We never collect location in the background. Your location is used on the device to show the map and to calculate distances. Only when you request walking directions are the origin and destination coordinates sent to a routing service (OpenRouteService). We do not store any location history on our servers.

### 2.4 Data kept only on your device

The following stay on your device and are never sent to our servers:

- Display language setting
- Your acceptance of this policy (the version accepted and the date and time of acceptance)
- Offline cache of evacuation shelter data

## 3. Why we use it

1. To provide and display tourist spot, evacuation shelter and etiquette information
2. To show the map, calculate distances and provide walking directions based on your location
3. To authenticate your account
4. To publish, display and manage reviews
5. To moderate inappropriate content and otherwise operate the service
6. To respond to your enquiries

## 4. Sharing and processors

We do not share personal data with third parties without your consent, except where required by law.

We use the following service providers to operate the App:

| Provider | Purpose | Information shared |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Supabase | Authentication | Email address, hashed password, Google account information |
| Amazon Web Services | Server and image hosting | Display name, icon image, reviews, access logs |
| OpenRouteService | Walking route calculation | Origin and destination coordinates |
| Google LLC | Google Sign-In and map display | Google account information, data needed to render maps |

## 5. International transfers

Supabase, OpenRouteService and Google LLC may process information on servers located outside Japan. By using the App you agree to these transfers.

You may request information about the data protection regimes of those countries and the safeguards our providers apply by contacting us at the address in section 1.

## 6. Retention

- Account information: retained until the account is deleted, and erased promptly upon a deletion request.
- Reviews: retained until the review or the account is deleted.
- Location data: not stored on our servers.
- On-device settings and consent records: retained on your device until you uninstall the App.

## 7. Your rights

You may request disclosure, correction, addition, deletion, suspension of use, or suspension of disclosure to third parties for the personal data we hold about you. Contact us using the details in section 1.

You can delete your account yourself from the settings screen in the App. Deleting the account erases your account information and the data held by our authentication provider.
